Academic Programme

Our Curriculum

01
Foundations of Economics
Microeconomics, macroeconomics, and the philosophical underpinnings of classical economic thought from Smith, Ricardo, and Mill.
Core
02
Indian Economic Thought
Arthashastra, Vedic economic philosophy, and the evolution of Indian economic policy from ancient to modern times.
Core
03
Quantitative Methods
Econometrics, statistical analysis, data interpretation, and mathematical modelling applied to real-world economic problems.
Elective
04
Public Policy & Governance
How fiscal and monetary policy is crafted, debated, and implemented — including RBI, Union Budget, and global institutions.
Elective
05
Behavioural Economics
Kahneman, Thaler, and the psychology of decision-making. Why humans deviate from rational models and what that means for markets.
Advanced
06
Seminars & Case Studies
Guest lectures, debate sessions, stock market simulations, budget analysis workshops, and inter-college competitions.
